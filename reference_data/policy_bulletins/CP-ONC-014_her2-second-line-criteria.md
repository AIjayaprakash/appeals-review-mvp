# Clinical Policy Bulletin CP-ONC-014
## HER2-Positive Breast Cancer — Second-Line Therapy Criteria (incl. Trastuzumab Deruxtecan)

**Effective date:** 2025-06-01 · **Applies to:** CPT/HCPCS J9358

### Coverage criteria

Second-line HER2-directed antibody-drug conjugate therapy (e.g., trastuzumab deruxtecan) is considered medically necessary when **all** of the following are met:

1. Confirmed HER2-positive status (IHC 3+, or IHC 2+ with confirmatory ISH amplification).
2. Documented disease progression during or after a first-line HER2-directed regimen (e.g., trastuzumab + pertuzumab + taxane), evidenced by imaging (RECIST criteria or equivalent) or unambiguous clinical progression.
3. Baseline left ventricular ejection fraction ≥ 50%, confirmed by echocardiogram or MUGA within 60 days of request, given known cardiotoxicity risk.
4. ECOG performance status of 0–2.
5. No untreated symptomatic brain metastases requiring immediate intervention (stable, treated CNS disease is not exclusionary).

### Common reasons for denial

- Progression on first-line therapy not confirmed by imaging or clearly documented clinical findings (most frequent reason for initial denial).
- Cardiac function assessment missing or outdated (>60 days).
- HER2 status confirmation not included in submission.

### Appeal guidance

Denials citing "alternative first-line options not yet exhausted" should be appealed with imaging confirming progression on the completed first-line regimen — plan policy does not require a member to try additional first-line-equivalent regimens once progression on standard first-line therapy is documented.
