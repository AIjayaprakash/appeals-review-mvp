# Clinical Policy Bulletin CP-PT-002
## Outpatient Physical Therapy — Benefit Allowance & Extension Criteria

**Effective date:** 2025-01-01 · **Applies to:** CPT 97110, 97112, 97140

### Standard allowance

Outpatient physical therapy is covered up to the member's plan-specific annual visit allowance (see member benefit summary; commonly 20–30 visits/year depending on plan). Prior authorization is required for the initial course of treatment.

### Section 1 — Post-operative therapy

Post-surgical physical therapy is covered as part of the standard annual allowance. No separate post-operative allowance exists outside the annual visit cap.

### Section 2 — Medical necessity for continued treatment within the allowance

Continued visits within the remaining allowance require documentation of measurable functional progress (e.g., improving range of motion, strength, or a validated functional outcome measure such as the Oswestry Disability Index for spine cases).

### Section 3 — Extension beyond the annual allowance

Extension beyond the member's annual visit allowance is considered medically necessary only when **all** of the following are documented:

1. A validated functional outcome measure (e.g., Oswestry Disability Index, FOTO) showing a clear, ongoing improvement trend — not merely continued symptoms.
2. A specific, time-bound functional goal that has not yet been met (e.g., "return to unrestricted lifting at work").
3. A treating-therapist attestation estimating the additional visit count required to reach that goal, with an expected discharge date.
4. Confirmation that the requested extension is not attributable to a gap in prior treatment adherence.

### Common reasons for denial

- Progress notes describe subjective improvement only, with no objective functional outcome measure.
- No specific functional goal or expected discharge point stated.
- Request exceeds the annual allowance without an explicit Section 3 justification.

### Appeal guidance

An appeal for visits beyond the annual allowance should be evaluated against Section 3 specifically — a request that only restates ongoing symptoms without an objective outcome measure and a bounded functional goal does not meet criteria, regardless of clinical plausibility.
