# Clinical Policy Bulletin CP-BARI-021
## Bariatric Surgery — Medical Necessity Criteria

**Effective date:** 2025-01-01 · **Applies to:** CPT 43644, 43645, 43775

### Coverage criteria

Bariatric surgery (including laparoscopic Roux-en-Y gastric bypass and sleeve gastrectomy) is considered medically necessary when **all** of the following are met:

1. Body mass index (BMI) ≥ 40, **or** BMI ≥ 35 with at least one qualifying obesity-related comorbidity (e.g., type 2 diabetes, hypertension, obstructive sleep apnea), documented within 6 months of the request.
2. Completion of a minimum 6-month physician-supervised weight-management program within the 2 years preceding the request, with documented participation dates and outcomes.
3. Psychological evaluation clearing the member for surgery, completed within 12 months of the request.
4. No untreated, uncontrolled endocrine disorder that could account for the obesity.

### Common reasons for denial

- BMI below the required threshold, with no qualifying comorbidity documented.
- Physician-supervised weight-management program shorter than 6 months, or completed outside the 2-year lookback window.
- No documented psychological evaluation on file.

### Appeal guidance

An appeal should address the specific criterion cited as unmet. Restating the original request without new BMI, comorbidity, or weight-management program documentation does not meet criteria, regardless of the surgical rationale offered.
