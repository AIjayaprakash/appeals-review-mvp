# Clinical Policy Bulletin CP-CARD-009
## Cardiac Resynchronization Therapy with Defibrillator (CRT-D) — Medical Necessity Criteria

**Effective date:** 2025-01-01 · **Applies to:** CPT 33249, 33224

### Coverage criteria

CRT-D implantation is considered medically necessary when **all** of the following are met:

1. Left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) ≤ 35%, confirmed by echocardiogram within 90 days of the request.
2. NYHA Class II, III, or ambulatory Class IV heart failure symptoms at the time of request.
3. QRS duration criteria, one of:
   - QRS ≥ 150 ms with left bundle branch block (LBBB) morphology, **or**
   - QRS 130–149 ms with LBBB morphology plus additional risk factors (e.g., non-ischemic cardiomyopathy), **or**
   - QRS ≥ 150 ms with non-LBBB morphology, supported by additional documentation of dyssynchrony.
4. A minimum 90-day trial of guideline-directed medical therapy (GDMT) — including a beta-blocker, an ACE inhibitor/ARB/ARNI, and a mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist as tolerated — with continued symptoms despite therapy. The GDMT trial period and specific agents/doses must be documented in the submission.
5. Life expectancy of at least 1 year with good functional status.

### Common reasons for denial

- Missing or incomplete GDMT trial documentation (most frequent reason for initial denial).
- LVEF or QRS duration not included in the submission, or measured more than 90 days prior to request.
- NYHA class not documented at the time of request.

### Appeal guidance

An appeal should directly address the specific criterion cited as unmet in the denial letter. Submitting the complete GDMT trial record (agent, dose, start date, and documented symptom persistence) is the single most common resolution for denials citing "insufficient GDMT documentation."
